The Freedoms Party has today announced its party list ahead of the next general election, unveiling the team it says will fight for a fairer and stronger Square Country.

Party Leader David Campbell said the list reflected the breadth of the movement and its determination to offer voters a serious alternative to the government.

"This is a team that understands the challenges facing Square Country and is ready to get to work."

David Campbell, Leader of the Freedoms Party

The party says its candidates come from a wide range of backgrounds and communities, bringing experience in public service, business, education, community organisations and advocacy.

The announcement comes as parties across Square Country begin preparing for the election campaign. Freedoms is positioning itself as the country's largest left-wing opposition party and has promised to campaign on its programme for a fairer economy, stronger public services and more affordable homes.

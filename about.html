Our history

A movement shaped by Square Country.

For generations, the Freedoms Party has stood for a fairer society, a stronger economy and a government that works for ordinary people.

The Freedoms Party

A long tradition of progressive politics.

The Freedoms Party is one of Square Country's major political movements. Throughout its history, the party has evolved alongside the country it represents.

From its early years as a reform movement to its modern role as the largest party of opposition, Freedoms has sought to give working people, families and communities a stronger voice in government.

While the issues facing Square Country have changed, the party's central belief has remained: government should improve people's lives and opportunity should not depend on wealth or circumstance.

Our history

The Freedoms story

The party's history is a story of changing times, political debate and a continuing commitment to giving people a voice.

01

THE BEGINNING

A party for reform

The Freedoms movement emerged from a growing demand for political reform and a government more responsive to the needs of ordinary Square Country citizens. Its early supporters brought together workers, reformers and community leaders.

02

GROWING INFLUENCE

Finding a national voice

As the party grew, Freedoms expanded its organisation beyond its traditional strongholds. Candidates began contesting electorates across Square Country, turning the party into a genuinely national political force.

03

GOVERNMENT

From opposition to office

Freedoms went on to form governments during periods when voters turned to the party for change. Its time in office helped shape many of the institutions and public services that remain important to Square Country today.

04

MODERN FREEDOMS

A new generation

A new generation of Freedoms MPs and activists has brought fresh ideas to the movement. The party has renewed its focus on economic fairness, public services, housing, communities and the environment.

05

TODAY

The opposition for Square Country

Today, Freedoms is the largest left-wing opposition party in Parliament. With six MPs and a growing nationwide organisation, we are preparing for the next chapter of our history.

What we stand for

Our policies.

A fairer economy. Stronger public services. Better opportunities. Our policies set out how a Freedoms government would build a better Square Country.

Our vision

Politics should deliver.

The Freedoms Party believes government should make people's lives better — not harder.

Our policies are built around a simple idea: Square Country works best when everyone has a fair chance to get ahead, when public services are there when people need them, and when government focuses on the things that matter.

Featured policy

We will end the War in Rivoria.

It has gone on long enough. A Freedoms Government will end the War in Rivoria as soon as it possibly can. Every effort will be made to end the conflict from day one.

Our housing policy

A home should be within reach.

We will build more homes, make renting fairer, expand social housing and help more people achieve the security of owning their own home.

Our position

Everyone deserves somewhere to call home.

Housing is more than bricks and mortar. A secure, affordable home gives families stability, allows communities to thrive and gives people the foundations to build their lives.

For too many people in Square Country, housing costs are taking up too much of their income while aspiring homeowners struggle to get onto the property ladder.

The Freedoms Party believes the answer is to build more homes, increase affordable housing supply and make sure renters and homeowners are treated fairly.

"A decent home should be a foundation for a good life — not a luxury reserved for the few."

— James Holloway, Housing Spokesperson

Our commitments

More homes. Fairer housing.

Our housing plan tackles affordability from every angle — from construction and home ownership to renting and social housing.

01

Build more homes

Square Country needs a sustained increase in housing construction. Freedoms will create a new programme called SquareBuild, and we aim to build 100,000 homes for Squares.

02

Help people own their home

Home ownership should not be an impossible dream. We will support first-home buyers and create pathways into ownership for more families.

03

Fairer renting

Renters deserve security and dignity. We will strengthen protections for tenants while maintaining a healthy supply of rental homes.

04

Expand social housing

Everyone deserves a safe place to live. We will expand the supply of quality social housing and ensure homes are available to those who need them most.

05

Build better communities

New housing must come with the infrastructure, schools, transport and public spaces that make communities good places to live.

06

Improve housing quality

A home should be warm, safe and healthy. We will work to improve housing standards and tackle poor-quality accommodation.

Home ownership

Giving more people a path to ownership.

Owning a home provides security and gives families a stake in their community. We want to make it possible for more working people to achieve that security.

Support first-home buyers

We will improve pathways for first-home buyers trying to save a deposit and purchase a home.

Increase affordable supply

The long-term solution to housing affordability is to build enough homes for the people who need them.

Plan for growing communities

New developments should have roads, schools, parks and services ready for the families moving in.

Build a stable housing market

We want a housing market that works for homeowners, renters, builders and communities.

Renters

Renting should mean having a home.

Not everyone is ready or able to buy a home. Renting should provide people with stability, security and a decent standard of living.

Security for tenants

Renters should have clear rights and reasonable security in their homes.

Fair standards

Rental properties should meet appropriate standards for safety, warmth and quality.

Manageable housing costs

We will focus on the underlying shortage of homes that drives rental costs higher.

Respect for landlords

A healthy rental market needs responsible landlords as well as strong tenant protections.

Social housing

A safety net that works.

Social housing should provide people facing hardship with a safe and secure place to live. We will increase supply and ensure existing homes are properly maintained.

Homelessness

Prevent homelessness.

The best response to homelessness is to prevent people from losing their homes in the first place. We will strengthen early support and housing services for people at risk.

Building communities

We don't just need more houses. We need better communities.

New housing should be part of a wider plan for thriving communities. Growth must be matched by investment in transport, schools, healthcare, recreation and public spaces.

01

Infrastructure

Roads, public transport, water and other essential infrastructure must keep pace with population growth.

02

Local services

Growing communities need schools, healthcare, shops and other essential services close to home.

03

Green spaces

Parks, public spaces and recreation areas help turn housing developments into places people are proud to call home.

Our promise

More homes. More opportunity. More security.

The Freedoms Party will build a housing system where more people can afford a home, renters have security and every community has room to thrive.

Our policies

Communities & government.

Putting power closer to people, strengthening local communities and building a government that listens, delivers and answers to the public.

Our position

Government should work for people.

Too often, decisions are made far away from the communities they affect. Local people know what their communities need, but governments can be slow to listen and even slower to act.

The Freedoms Party believes in a government that trusts people, strengthens communities and gives local leaders the tools they need to get things done.

We want public institutions that are transparent, accountable and focused on delivering results — not creating unnecessary bureaucracy.

"Strong communities are the foundation of a strong Square Country."

— Anna Corfield, Communities & Government Spokesperson

Our commitments

A government closer to you.

We will give communities more control, make government more accountable and put delivery back at the centre of public service.

01

Give communities a stronger voice

We will strengthen local decision-making and ensure communities have a meaningful say in the policies and projects that affect them.

02

Make government more accountable

Government should explain its decisions, publish information openly and be held responsible when promises are not delivered.

03

Amend the Electoral Act 1986

We will amend the Electoral Act 1986 to increase the number of MPs from 26 to 34, giving Square Country more representation in Parliament.

04

Cut unnecessary red tape

We will simplify government processes so people, charities and businesses can spend less time navigating bureaucracy.

05

Work with Westland (Tierras del oeste)

A Freedoms Government will work with the Westland (Tierras del oeste) Council, in hopes of securing a long-term relationship with the region. Freedoms won't throw them out like the Liberty Government.

06

Modernise public services

Government services should be simple to access, easy to understand and designed around the people who use them.

What will change

A government that gets things done.

Decisions closer to communities

More decisions should be made by people who understand the communities they affect.

Greater transparency

Public money and public decisions should be visible to the people who fund them.

Less bureaucracy

We will identify outdated rules and processes that make it harder to get things done.

Better public services

Services should be built around people's needs rather than the structure of government.

Stronger places

Every community matters.

Vibrant local communities

We will support local initiatives, community groups and projects that make neighbourhoods stronger and more connected.

Better local infrastructure

Roads, public spaces, community facilities and essential infrastructure should be maintained properly and planned for the future.

Supporting volunteers

Volunteers and community organisations do enormous amounts of work. Government should help them, not burden them with unnecessary administration.

A stronger public voice

People deserve genuine opportunities to participate in decisions that affect their homes, families and communities.

Our promise

Government should serve the people — not the other way around.

The Freedoms Party will put communities at the centre of government and give people a stronger voice in the future of their country.

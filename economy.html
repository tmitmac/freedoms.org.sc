Our economic policy

A fair economy.

An economy that rewards work, supports enterprise and gives every person a genuine opportunity to build a better life.

Our position

Growth should mean a better life.

Square Country needs an economy that works for everyone — not just those who are already doing well.

The Freedoms Party believes economic growth and fairness go together. We want people to keep more of what they earn, businesses to have the confidence to invest and create jobs, and government to spend taxpayers' money responsibly.

Our approach is straightforward: make it easier to work, easier to build, easier to invest and easier to get ahead.

"A strong economy isn't measured only by what government collects. It's measured by what people are able to achieve."

— David Thornfield, Finance Spokesperson

Our commitments

An economy that works for you.

Our economic programme focuses on household incomes, opportunity, enterprise and responsible government.

01

Make work pay

People who work hard should be able to see the benefits in their pay packet. We will focus on lifting household incomes and rewarding employment.

02

Support small business

Small businesses are at the heart of local economies. We will reduce unnecessary compliance costs and make it easier for businesses to grow.

03

Tackle the cost of living

Families should not have to choose between essentials. We will address the pressures that make everyday life more expensive.

04

Responsible taxation

Taxpayers deserve a system that is fair, understandable and competitive while providing government with the revenue it genuinely needs.

05

Invest for growth

Better infrastructure, innovation and investment will help Square Country become more productive and competitive.

06

Introduce a Capital Gains Tax (CGT)

To pay for the services all Squares deserve, a CGT will increase government revenue, allowing more to be spent on crucial services.

07

Repeal the Defence Expansion Act 2025

The last thing we need to be spending money on right now is defence. We need to spend it on Squares. Freedoms will repeal the DEA and will not replace it.

07

Increase Income Tax for Wealthy

Freedoms will increase the top tax brackets so that the wealthy pay their share.

For working people

More money in your pocket.

The economy is not an abstract number on a government spreadsheet. It is the wages people take home, the rent they pay, the groceries they buy and the opportunities available to their children.

Higher household incomes

Policies that encourage employment, investment and stronger wages.

Lower everyday pressures

Action on the costs that put pressure on household budgets.

More opportunities to work

A labour market where people can find work, develop skills and progress.

A growing economy

Long-term growth that creates jobs, investment and opportunity.

Enterprise

Backing the people who build.

Small business

Small and medium businesses should spend their time serving customers and creating jobs, not filling out unnecessary forms.

Innovation

We will encourage innovation, new industries and investment in the technologies that will shape Square Country's future.

International trade

Square Country should be ambitious in the global economy and create opportunities for local businesses to reach new markets.

Productivity

Better infrastructure, skills and technology can help workers and businesses produce more and earn more.

Responsible government

Every dollar matters.

Government does not have its own money. It spends money collected from people and businesses through taxation. That means every dollar should be treated with respect.

01

Value for money

Government programmes should demonstrate clear results and genuine public value.

02

Sustainable finances

Today's decisions should not leave future generations carrying unnecessary costs.

03

Transparency

People have a right to understand where their money goes and what government achieves with it.

Our promise

A stronger economy. A fairer Square Country.

We believe Square Country can be more prosperous, more productive and more fair — and that economic growth should improve the lives of ordinary people.

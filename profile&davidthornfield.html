About David

Fighting for Masterton Central

Born in Square City, in 1980, David Thornfield is the nephew of former Freedoms Prime Minister Clara E. Thornfield. He grew up in the suburb of Meston, attending South Meston Hills High School, before moving to Elizabeth University of Queenstown. It was there where he became an outspoken member of the Freedoms Party. In 2023, Thornfield was elected to Parliament as the MP for Masterton Central, representing the city and its surroundings in Parliament and working to deliver a fairer future for Square Country.

As a member of the opposition, Thornfield works alongside leader David Campbell, helping to campaign on a fairer future for all Squares. Thornfield hopes to follow in the footsteps of his aunt Clara, who became one of the most prominent leaders in Square Country history.

In 2026, Thornfield accepted the candidacy for the seat of Masterton Central, where he hopes to secure a second term—this time in government.

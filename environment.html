Our environment policy

Protecting the place we call home.

We will protect Square Country's natural environment, invest in clean energy and make our communities more resilient for generations to come.

Our position

A cleaner, healthier future.

The environment we inherit is one of Square Country's greatest assets. From our coastlines and forests to our rivers, farmland and cities, we have a responsibility to protect it.

The Freedoms Party believes environmental action should improve people's lives as well as protect the natural world. Cleaner energy, better public transport and healthier communities can go hand in hand with a stronger economy.

We will take practical action to reduce pollution, protect nature and prepare Square Country for the environmental challenges ahead.

"We do not have to choose between a strong economy and a healthy environment. We can build both."

— Max Kingswell, Environment Spokesperson

Our commitments

Practical action for our environment.

Our environmental plan focuses on clean energy, conservation, resilient communities and reducing pollution.

01

Clean energy

We are committing to 100% clean, renewable energy

02

Protect our natural environment

We will protect native forests, waterways, coastlines and habitats while strengthening conservation across Square Country.

03

Cleaner communities

Cleaner streets, healthier waterways and better waste management will help make our towns and cities healthier places to live.

04

Climate resilience

We will prepare communities for environmental risks by investing in resilient infrastructure and long-term planning.

05

Sustainable transport

Better public transport, walking and cycling infrastructure will give people cleaner and more practical ways to get around.

06

A circular economy

We will reduce waste, encourage recycling and support businesses developing products and systems that use resources more efficiently.

Clean energy

Cleaner energy. Stronger economy.

The transition to cleaner energy is an opportunity to create jobs, attract investment and build a more secure energy system for Square Country.

Invest in clean generation

Support new clean energy generation and the infrastructure required to deliver reliable electricity.

Strengthen energy security

A cleaner energy system must also be dependable and affordable for households and businesses.

More efficient homes

Better energy efficiency can reduce household costs while making homes warmer and healthier.

Create green jobs

New industries and technologies can create skilled jobs and economic opportunities across Square Country.

Conservation

Protecting our natural heritage.

Square Country's natural environment belongs to all of us. Protecting it means protecting the places and ecosystems that future generations will inherit.

Restore native ecosystems

Support restoration of native habitats and strengthen protection for threatened ecosystems.

Protect our waterways

Improve the health of rivers, lakes and coastal waters and reduce harmful pollution.

Protect native forests

Safeguard important native forests and support responsible land management.

Better access to nature

Protect parks and public spaces so people can enjoy the natural environment close to where they live.

Waste

Less waste. More reuse.

We will reduce unnecessary waste and encourage recycling, reuse and better resource management across households, businesses and government.

Pollution

Cleaner air. Cleaner water.

We will tackle sources of pollution that affect people's health and the quality of the environment, with practical action at local and national level.

A resilient Square Country

Preparing our communities for the future.

Environmental challenges can affect homes, infrastructure, businesses and communities. Government must plan ahead rather than wait for problems to become crises.

01

Resilient infrastructure

Invest in infrastructure that can withstand environmental pressures and protect essential services.

02

Better planning

Make long-term environmental risks part of decisions about housing, transport and development.

03

Stronger communities

Give councils and communities the resources and support they need to prepare for environmental challenges.

Our promise

A cleaner country. A stronger future.

The Freedoms Party will protect our environment, support cleaner energy and make sure Square Country is ready for the challenges of tomorrow.

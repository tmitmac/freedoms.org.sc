Our health policy

Healthcare for everyone.

A stronger public health system where people can see a doctor, access treatment and receive the care they need — when they need it.

Our position

Healthcare is a public right.

The Freedoms Party believes that your ability to receive quality healthcare should not depend on your income, your postcode or your circumstances.

Our public health system should be properly supported, well managed and accessible to every person in Square Country.

We will focus on reducing waiting times, improving access to primary care, supporting our healthcare workforce and making sure hospitals have the resources they need.

"When you need healthcare, you shouldn't have to wonder whether you can get it."

— Lisa Bancroft, Health Spokesperson

Our commitments

A health system that puts people first.

Our plan focuses on access, capacity and quality — making sure the system is there when people need it.

01

Cut hospital waiting times

We will increase hospital capacity and focus resources on reducing unnecessary delays for patients waiting for appointments, procedures and treatment.

02

Strengthen primary care

Better access to GPs, nurses and community health services can help people get treatment earlier and reduce pressure on hospitals.

03

Back our healthcare workers

Doctors, nurses, paramedics, carers and other health professionals deserve safe workplaces, proper support and the resources to do their jobs.

04

Improve mental healthcare

Mental healthcare should be treated as an essential part of healthcare, with accessible services for people who need support.

05

Modernise hospitals

We will invest in hospital facilities, equipment and technology so healthcare professionals have the tools they need.

06

Proceed with the Queenstown City Hospital

We will invest in the Queenstown City Hospital that was put on indefinite hold by the Liberty Government.

07

Focus on prevention

Prevention and early intervention can keep people healthier and reduce pressure on the health system over the long term.

07

Proceed with the Queenstown City Hospital

Freedoms will proceed with the Queenstown City Hospital (QCH). For too long now, Queenstownians have had to put up with inadequate healthcare. Freedoms will end that with the construction of the QCH.

Access

Care when you need it.

Access to healthcare should not depend on where you live or whether you can afford to wait. We will make access and timely treatment a central measure of health system performance.

Shorter waits

Patients should receive appointments, diagnostics and treatment within reasonable and transparent timeframes.

Better regional healthcare

People outside major centres deserve access to high-quality healthcare close to home.

Easier access to primary care

We will support services that help people access appropriate care before conditions become more serious.

Care based on need

Healthcare decisions should be based on clinical need and patient wellbeing.

Our workforce

Backing the people who care for us.

Recruit and retain staff

Square Country needs enough doctors, nurses, midwives, allied health professionals and support staff to meet demand.

Value expertise

Healthcare workers should have a meaningful voice in how services are designed and delivered.

Safer workplaces

Healthcare workers deserve safe, respectful workplaces where they can focus on caring for patients.

Train the next generation

We will support education and training pathways that build a strong healthcare workforce for the future.

Mental health

Mental health is health.

Too many people struggle to access timely mental health support. We will make mental healthcare a core part of a stronger public health system.

Earlier support, better outcomes

We will expand access to community-based support, improve pathways into treatment and strengthen services for people experiencing serious mental health challenges.

Our approach

Accessible. Early. Compassionate. Evidence-based.

Prevention

Keeping people healthy.

The best health system is one that helps people stay healthy in the first place. Prevention can improve lives while reducing pressure on hospitals and emergency services.

01

Early intervention

Identify health problems earlier and help people access treatment before conditions worsen.

02

Healthy communities

Support community-based programmes that help people make healthier choices and stay active.

03

Better information

Give people clear, reliable information so they can make informed decisions about their health.

Our promise

A health system people can rely on.

The Freedoms Party will build a stronger public health system that puts patients first, backs healthcare workers and delivers care when people need it.

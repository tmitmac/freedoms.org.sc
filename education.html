Our education policy

Every student deserves a chance.

A public education system that gives every young person the knowledge, skills and opportunities they need to build a successful future.

Our position

Education is the foundation.

Every child and young person deserves access to a high-quality education, regardless of their background, circumstances or where they live.

The Freedoms Party believes schools should have the resources they need, teachers should be supported and students should be encouraged to reach their full potential.

We will invest in the people and institutions that make education work — while giving students multiple pathways into further study, training and employment.

"Where you start in life should never determine how far you can go."

— David Campbell, Education Spokesperson

Our commitments

A better education system.

Our education plan puts students first while supporting the teachers and schools that help them succeed.

01

Invest in our schools

Schools need modern facilities, appropriate resources and reliable funding to give every student the education they deserve.

02

Back our teachers

Teachers are at the heart of education. We will support them with better resources, professional development and manageable workloads.

03

Raise educational achievement

We will focus on strong foundations in reading, writing, mathematics and critical thinking while ensuring students have a broad education.

04

Support every learner

Students have different needs. Schools should have the support and specialist services required to help every learner participate and succeed.

05

Replace the National Diploma of Education

It's simple: NDE isn't working for students. We will replace it with a results-proven education system that will work for all students, giving them the education they need to succeed in life.

06

Stronger vocational pathways

University is not the only route to a successful career. We will strengthen apprenticeships, trades and practical training opportunities.

07

Education beyond school

Further education, universities and lifelong learning should give people the opportunity to gain new skills throughout their lives.

Teachers

Great schools need great teachers.

Teachers should be able to spend their time doing what they entered the profession to do: teaching. We will reduce unnecessary administrative pressure and support teachers to succeed.

Better professional development

Teachers should have access to high-quality training throughout their careers.

Less unnecessary paperwork

We will look for ways to reduce unnecessary administration and return teachers' time to the classroom.

More support staff

Schools should have access to the specialist staff they need to support students and teachers.

Respect the profession

Education policy should recognise the expertise and professionalism of teachers.

Students

Putting students first.

Education should prepare young people for the real world while giving them the knowledge, confidence and support to succeed.

Strong foundations

Every student should leave primary education with strong literacy and numeracy skills.

Student wellbeing

Schools should be safe environments where students can learn, grow and ask for help.

Digital skills

Students need the digital literacy and critical thinking skills required in a modern economy.

Real-world opportunities

Schools should connect students with employers, training providers and further education.

Vocational education

More pathways into work.

Apprenticeships, trades and vocational education provide valuable pathways into rewarding careers. We will work to make these options more accessible and better connected with employers.

Higher education

Opportunity after school.

Universities and other tertiary institutions are essential to our economy and society. We will support access to high-quality further education and the skills our country needs.

Equal opportunity

Your postcode shouldn't decide your future.

Students in every part of Square Country deserve access to excellent schools and opportunities. We will work to address educational disadvantage and ensure resources reach the communities that need them most.

01

Regional schools

Strong schools should be available to students outside the largest cities.

02

Support disadvantaged students

Additional support should reach students facing barriers to participation and achievement.

03

Equal opportunity

Every student should have the opportunity to discover their strengths and determine their own future.

Our promise

Every child. Every opportunity.

The Freedoms Party will build an education system that gives students the opportunity to succeed, supports teachers and strengthens Square Country for generations to come.
